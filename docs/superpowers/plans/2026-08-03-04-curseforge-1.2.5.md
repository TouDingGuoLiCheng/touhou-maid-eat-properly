```
### Touhou Maid: Eat properly — 1.2.5

Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Requires Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3+

#### Highlights

**Snack raid (“find food yourself”)**
- When a maid is hungry and has no edible food in her backpack, she may quietly walk to a nearby chest / snack cabinet, wait, take one food item, restore satiety, then run away.
- Does **not** complete a food request. Success lines are **bubble-only** (no public chat spam).
- Toggle per maid on the **Eat properly** tab (cookie button next to the satiety label): allow “find food yourself” or force “wait for owner”.
- Can be disabled globally in config (`snackRaidEnabled`). Search radius, cooldown, and speech lists are configurable.

**Request delivery tightened**
- Completing a request now requires **active Lunchbox / Dual Lunchbox delivery**.
- Putting food into the maid’s inventory GUI no longer auto-completes the request.
- Dual Lunchbox: when both slots are filled with food-pool items and at least one matches the current request, completing grants **+2 favorability levels**.

**Care items & speech**
- Hypnosis Note: clears the current request only (no favor gain, no thank-you / completion speech).
- Extra Meal Permit / Hypnosis Note lines send **immediately** to the owner (not deferred to the daytime chat queue).
- Public chat timing follows each maid’s **work schedule** (day / night / all-day), instead of a fixed global window. Bubbles still appear right away.

**Satiety & meals**
- Work/home meals only start when satiety is below `eatWhenSatietyBelow` (default **18**).
- When satiety is below `hungryBingeUntilSatiety` (default **8**), she may binge past the normal meal cooldown until she reaches that value.
- Harmful foods (e.g. poisonous potato effects) are filtered out of the request food pool.

**UI fixes**
- Player trinket tab button is visible on **survival and creative** inventory screens (near the player model).

#### Config notes

After editing `config/touhou_maid_eat_properly-common.toml`, rejoin the world / restart the server.
New keys under `[snackRaid]` and meal thresholds above are available in this build.

#### Dependencies

- Required: Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3+
- Optional: Farmer’s Delight, Youkai’s Homecoming (larger food pool / Lake Fairy Keepsake trade bonus)

### 车万女仆：好好吃饭 — 1.2.5

Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · 需要车万女仆 1.5.3+

#### 亮点

**自己找吃的（偷吃）**
- 女仆饿了且背包没有可吃的食物时，可能悄悄走到附近箱子 / 零食柜，停一会儿后取走一份食物补饱食，再跑开。
- **不算**完成索要；成功时只有头顶气泡，不刷公屏。
- 在「好好吃饭」页、饱食标签旁的饼干按钮可按女仆开关：「自己找」或「等主人」。
- 也可在配置里关闭整段功能（`snackRaidEnabled`）；搜索半径、冷却、话术均可改。

**索要完成方式收紧**
- 完成索要改为用**送餐盒 / 双层送餐盒**主动交付。
- 往女仆 GUI 背包里塞食物**不会**再自动完成索要。
- 双层送餐盒：两格都装满且属于食物池、至少一格对上当前索要时，一次可 **+2 好等级**。

**养成道具与聊天**
- 催眠便签：只清除当前索要（不加好感、不播感谢 / 完成话术）。
- 加餐许可 / 催眠便签等道具话术会对主人**立刻公屏**，不再塞进白天延迟队列。
- 公屏可发送时段改为跟随每只女仆的**排班**（白班 / 夜班 / 全天）；气泡仍立刻出现。

**饱食与进食**
- 工作餐 / 家餐仅在饱食低于 `eatWhenSatietyBelow`（默认 **18**）时才会主动吃。
- 饱食低于 `hungryBingeUntilSatiety`（默认 **8**）时，可忽略常规进食冷却、连吃到该值。
- 索要食物池会剔除带负面效果的食物（如毒马铃薯一类）。

**界面修复**
- 玩家饰品栏按钮在**生存 / 创造**背包界面均可看见（靠近玩家模型预览）。

#### 配置说明

修改 `config/touhou_maid_eat_properly-common.toml` 后请重新进档 / 重启服务端。  
本版本新增 `[snackRaid]` 相关项，以及上述进食门槛配置。

#### 依赖

- 必需：车万女仆 1.5.3+
- 可选：农夫乐事、妖怪的归家（扩大索要食物池；湖之冰精信物交易加成）
```
